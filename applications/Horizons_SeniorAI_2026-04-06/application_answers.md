## Describe your experience deploying LLMs and RAG solutions in production
The Virtual Analyst Platform handles end-to-end LLM and RAG workflows in production. I built the retrieval layer over Azure AI Search with custom chunking, embedding, and re-ranking strategies tuned to financial documents. The generation layer uses LangGraph-based agents with tool use, structured output, and human-in-the-loop escalation for edge cases. I manage observability through custom eval pipelines that monitor retrieval quality, output correctness, and latency, and I use LiteLLM for unified model routing across OpenAI, Gemini, and Claude. The system has been live in a regulated financial environment since launch with no major production incidents.
