06 In My Own Words

Interview Q&A

Q“Tell me about yourself.”

I'm a Senior AI Engineer with a background in CS and finance. At PSP Investments (Sep 2022 – Aug 2026) I owned production agentic AI for institutional investment teams — the Virtual Analyst Platform from POC to production. Full-stack ownership: SDK, UI, onboarding, and roadmap. I also ship custom digithings applications for desks: sit with the team, ship on their stack, leave them the code. Based in Italy; open to contract.

Q“Why AI? Why finance + AI specifically?”

I came at it from both sides. CS gave me the engineering foundation; finance gave me the domain context to understand what analysts actually need. That mix has been useful for building tools people trust and use day to day.

  • Finance is demanding for AI: high stakes, messy data, sophisticated users who need to verify everything
  • That environment pushed me to focus on reliability, traceability, and making outputs verifiable
  • I'm interested in applying those patterns to other industries where the stakes and impact are high
Q“Biggest technical challenge you've solved?”

Source traceability. Analysts can't take an AI's word for something. I built a system that links every claim back to its exact document, page, and passage, surfaced in the UI so they can verify without leaving the platform. Without that trust layer, the tool wouldn't get used at all.

Structured data integration. Financial datasets are too large for context windows. I built a backend that lets the model reference tables by path, work with compact summaries, and run ML analysis on that layer. The same pain points exist in healthcare, legal, and other data-heavy domains.

Q“Where do you see yourself in 5 years?”

I'm passionate about building AI systems in production that matter, and I want to keep learning and growing my expertise in the field. I'm open to any form of remote work; what matters to me is the work itself, the team, the value we ship, and the impact it has on clients.
