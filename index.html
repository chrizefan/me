06 In my own words

Interview Q&A

Q“Tell me about yourself.”

I'm a Senior AI Engineer at the intersection of computer science and finance. I ramp up quickly, understand what clients need, and work full stack with agents throughout. Most of my time now goes into digithings, Olympus on digiquant, DigiChat releases, and freelance builds on that stack. Before that, at PSP Investments, a large Canadian pension fund, I owned the Virtual Analyst Platform through proof of concept, deployment, and years of production use with active users.

Q“Why AI? Why finance + AI specifically?”

I've spent years building AI where the stakes are high and the users are sophisticated. They need to verify everything. That environment shaped how I work: reliability, source traceability, and tools people actually trust day to day.

  • Finance is demanding for AI: messy data, regulated workflows, users who won't accept black-box answers
  • That pushed me to focus on production systems: observability, grounding, and shipping under real constraints
  • I'm interested in applying those patterns anywhere the stakes and impact are high
Q“Biggest technical challenge you've solved?”

Source traceability. Analysts can't take an AI's word for something. I built a system that links every claim back to its exact document, page, and passage, surfaced in the UI so they can verify without leaving the platform. Without that trust layer, the tool wouldn't get used at all.

Structured data integration. Financial datasets are too large for context windows. I built a backend that lets the model reference tables by path, work with compact summaries, and run ML analysis on that layer. The same pain points exist in healthcare, legal, and other data-heavy domains.

Q“Where do you see yourself in 5 years?”

I want to keep owning production AI systems that matter: harder domains, deeper technical work, and teams that care about shipping something people actually use. Contract and remote are fine when the work is right.
